### 2. Report a Transit Alert Discrepancy

Our autonomous pipeline ingests live GTFS-RT alerts every 15 minutes. However, transit systems are highly dynamic, and rapid track changes or dispatch adjustments can occasionally result in a temporary lag.

If you spot an alert mismatch between Smooth Commutes and live station conditions:

1. **Send an Email**: [reece@reecewilliams.co](mailto:reece@reecewilliams.co) with the subject line `Alert Discrepancy: [Line Name]`.
2. **Include Key Details**:
   - City and Train Line (e.g., *NYC 1 Train* or *Chicago Blue Line*)
   - Station and Direction of travel (e.g., *South Ferry-bound at 72nd St*)
   - Time of observation and what was announced on-platform vs. shown on the site.
3. **Investigation**: We verify discrepancy reports against raw agency GTFS-RT logs to diagnose feed latency or API parser bugs.

### 3. Immediate Emergencies & Official Transit Agency Contacts

> [!CAUTION]
> **Emergency Safety Notice**:
> Smooth Commutes is an informational web application, not a transit dispatch or emergency service. 
> 
> If you are experiencing a life-threatening medical emergency, witnessing active crime, or observing a track-level fire or hazard, **call 911 immediately** or use the station emergency call boxes located on all subway and metro platforms.

For direct customer service, lost and found, or transit police assistance, contact the respective transit agency directly:

#### Metropolitan Transportation Authority (MTA — NYC Subway)
- **Customer Information Hotline**: Dial `511` (within NY State) or `(877) 690-5114`
- **MTA Police Department (Emergency)**: Dial `911` or call `(888) 682-7362`
- **MTA Police Text Line**: `(877) 682-8398`
- **Online Customer Assistance**: [new.mta.info/contact-us](https://new.mta.info/contact-us)

#### Chicago Transit Authority (CTA — Chicago 'L')
- **Customer Information**: `(888) 968-7282` (888-YOUR-CTA)
- **Hearing Impaired (TTY)**: `(888) 282-8891`
- **CTA Safety & Security**: [transitchicago.com/security](https://www.transitchicago.com/security/)
- **Online Customer Service**: [transitchicago.com/feedback](https://www.transitchicago.com/feedback/)

#### Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority (WMATA — Metrorail)
- **Customer Service**: `(202) 637-7000` (Mon–Fri 7am–8pm, Sat–Sun 8:30am–5pm)
- **Metro Transit Police (Emergency)**: `(202) 962-2121`
- **Metro Transit Police (Text Line)**: `MY-MTPD (69-6873)`
- **Online Customer Service**: [wmata.com/about/contact](https://www.wmata.com/about/contact/)
