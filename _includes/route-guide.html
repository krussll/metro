Schedule & Train Frequency

{% if page.categories contains 'nyc' %}

The NYC Subway operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. Train arrival frequencies vary based on peak commuting hours, off-peak midday travel, and late-night maintenance windows:

  • Weekday Rush Hours (6:30 AM – 9:30 AM & 3:30 PM – 8:00 PM): Trains typically arrive every 2 to 5 minutes.
  • Midday & Evenings: Trains arrive every 6 to 10 minutes.
  • Late Nights (12:00 AM – 5:00 AM): Headways extend to every 12 to 20 minutes. Planned track maintenance frequently reroutes trains or requires express/local bypass tracks during overnight hours.
  • Weekends: Headways typically range from 8 to 12 minutes, subject to planned weekend capital construction.

Key Transfer Hubs & Intermodal Connections

Planning connections across the {{ page.city }} transit network allows riders to reach regional rail, airport shuttles, and connecting lines:

{% if page.categories contains 'nyc' %}

Major subway transfer stations connect you to connecting subway lines and regional transit providers:

  • Midtown Manhattan Transfer Centers: Connect at Times Square-42 St / Port Authority Bus Terminal (A, C, E, N, Q, R, W, 1, 2, 3, 7, S) and Grand Central-42 St (4, 5, 6, 7, S, Metro-North Railroad, Long Island Rail Road via Grand Central Madison).

Fares & Contactless Payment

{% if page.categories contains 'nyc' %}

New York City Transit features the OMNY Contactless Fare System:

  • Tap-to-Pay: Simply tap your contactless credit card, debit card, smartphone (Apple Pay / Google Wallet), or physical OMNY card at any subway turnstile.
  • Base Fare: $2.90 per ride.
  • Weekly Fare Capping: Once you pay for 12 rides with the same device or card within a Monday-to-Sunday window, all subsequent rides for the remainder of that week are automatically free.
  • Free Transfers: One free transfer is automatically granted between subways and local buses within a 2-hour window.
{% elsif page.categories contains 'chi' %}

The Chicago Transit Authority utilizes the Ventra System:

  • Tap-to-Pay: Tap any contactless bank card, Apple Pay / Google Wallet, or reloadable Ventra Card directly at station turnstiles.
  • Base Rail Fare: $2.50 per ride.
  • Unlimited Passes: 1-Day, 3-Day, 7-Day, and 30-Day unlimited ride passes can be purchased at station vending kiosks or within the Ventra mobile application.
{% elsif page.categories contains 'dc' %}

Metrorail fares are handled electronically via the SmarTrip System:

  • Payment: Use a physical SmarTrip plastic card or add a digital SmarTrip card to your Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. You must tap both when entering and when exiting the system.
  • Distance & Time-Based Fares: Fares vary based on distance traveled and time of day, ranging from $2.00 during off-peak hours up to $6.00 maximum for long-distance peak commutes.
  • Bus-to-Rail Discount: Transfers between Metrobus and Metrorail receive an automatic $2.00 discount within a 2-hour window.
{% endif %}

Daily Commuter Tips for the {{ page.route }} Line

{% if page.categories contains 'nyc' %}
  • Boarding Positioning: For rapid station exits, look for overhead directional signs on the platform indicating whether turnstiles or transfers are at the train's front, middle, or rear.
  • Weekend Construction Nuances: Track work frequently causes local trains to run on express tracks or bypass stations in one direction. Always verify the live status above before boarding during weekend hours.
  • Late Night Riding: If traveling between midnight and 5:00 AM, board the car with the conductor (indicated by an active zebra-striped board in the middle of the platform) or the front car near the train operator.
{% elsif page.categories contains 'chi' %}
  • Loop Navigation: When riding trains through the Loop elevated structure, listen carefully to conductor announcements as trains turn onto different sides of the elevated rectangle (Wells, Van Buren, Wabash, and Lake).
  • Luggage & Rush Hour: If heading to or from the airport with suitcases, board the center train cars where standee areas provide wider floor clearance without blocking doors.
  • Elevator Status: Before traveling with wheelchairs or strollers, check live elevator operating conditions to avoid unexpected station closures.
{% elsif page.categories contains 'dc' %}
  • Tap In and Tap Out: Unlike flat-fare systems, Metrorail calculates fares upon exit. Always ensure your mobile phone or SmarTrip card remains accessible and charged throughout your journey.
  • Stand Right, Walk Left: A sacred D.C. commuter rule: when riding station escalators, stand to the right and allow passing passengers to walk on the left.
  • Track Work Single-Tracking: Scheduled weekend capital track work often requires trains to share a single track through construction zones, extending wait times to 15–20 minutes. Check the alert box above before heading into the station.
{% endif %}
