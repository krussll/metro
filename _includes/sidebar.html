We monitor live GTFS-RT feeds and transit agency APIs to provide real-time alerts across three major metropolitan transit networks:

MTA (New York)

The Metropolitan Transportation Authority manages the New York City subway system, operating 24/7 service across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, and Staten Island.

CTA (Chicago)

The Chicago Transit Authority operates the nation's second-largest public transportation system, running the iconic Chicago 'L' rapid transit network across 8 rail lines.

WMATA (Washington D.C.)

The Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority operates the Metrorail network serving millions of commuters across Washington D.C., Maryland, and Virginia.
