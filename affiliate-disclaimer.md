**Smooth Commutes** is committed to absolute transparency regarding how this platform operates, how it is funded, and how we protect editorial integrity.

---

### 1. Editorial Independence & Non-Sponsored Transit Data

The core mission of Smooth Commutes is to provide fast, reliable, and unadulterated public transit alerts across the NYC Subway, Chicago 'L', and Washington Metrorail.

- **Zero Commercial Alteration**: Service alerts, status badges, delay advisories, and planned weekend disruption schedules are **never sponsored, paid for, or influenced by third parties**.
- **Official Ingestion**: All line statuses are parsed verbatim from official municipal transit agency open feeds (MTA, CTA, and WMATA).
- **No Paid Placement**: No transportation provider, private shuttle service, rideshare company, or commercial advertiser can pay to prioritize or alter line status reports.

---

### 2. How Smooth Commutes is Funded

Smooth Commutes is a free public utility. To support server infrastructure, domain hosting, and autonomous API ingestion maintenance, the site relies on:

1. **Digital Advertising**: We display contextual digital advertisements (such as Google AdSense). Advertisements are clearly separated from transit content and marked as sponsored or promotional.
2. **Affiliate Advertising Programs**: When we recommend commuter equipment, transit accessories, or navigation tools, we may earn an affiliate commission on qualifying purchases:
   - **Amazon Associates**: We are a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.
   - **No Surcharge to Users**: Clicking an affiliate link does not cost you any extra money. Prices remain identical to what you would pay navigating directly to the retailer.
